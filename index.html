Case study

The estimating system I designed and built at work.

From paper quotes to a signed sale on site

Design, build & rollout · 2025–2026 · Laravel, React, TypeScript

Every quote used to be written on paper, and nobody knew during the visit whether a door was even available in that size. I replaced the paper with one tablet flow that runs in the customer's home, from the first measurement to the signature.

  1. 01Site survey
  2. 02Opening builder
  3. 03Pricing
  4. 04Estimate versions
  5. 05Presentation
  6. 06Signature & sale

Known before you leave

Each opening is checked as it's entered: whether the door comes in that size, what it costs, and whether it needs shop work. Problems surface in the customer's home, not after the order is placed.

One price, everywhere

The pricing engine applies each location's own rules, discounts and tax, so the number on the tablet is the number on the order.

Built to close

Salespeople can offer versions side by side, present them, then take an e-signature and payment. Finalizing creates the sale, so nothing is typed in twice.

I designed the flow in Figma and wrote the documentation before building it, then rolled it out with training videos, in-app product tours and release notes. It's now how quotes are done across 87 locations.
